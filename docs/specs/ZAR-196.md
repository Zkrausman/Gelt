# 🔒 ZAR-196 Atomic Shadow Capital Reservation

## 🎯 What

Shadow entries now reserve aggregate paper capital in the same path-locked transaction that appends the `OPEN` journal record. A reservation succeeds only when the requested positive, finite `AmountUSD` fits the remaining positive, finite `TotalCapitalUSD`; concurrent `Store` instances cannot pass a stale capacity check and collectively over-allocate the journal.

A legacy invalid/open-over-budget journal latches a durable, journal-adjacent capital-integrity state before a new entry is accepted. While latched, the engine continues mark-to-market and liquidation processing, converts every BUY to HOLD without a BUY alert, and records `capital_reservation_degraded: true` in persisted cycle and daemon telemetry. Promotion evaluation returns no candidates while the latch exists.

The latch survives restarts and cannot clear automatically. With explicit acknowledgement, rebaseline durably copies the non-empty canonical journal, records a pending halt, then creates a fresh empty journal and records archive provenance. Any partial failure remains pending and halted; missing or already-empty journals are refused. A crash after the clean completion record but before intent removal also remains halted: the marker is deliberately not auto-cleared because a failed directory sync is indistinguishable from that crash window; audited operator reconciliation is required.

```text
gelt shadow capital-rebaseline --acknowledge-empty-journal-rebaseline
```

The bridge no longer writes synthetic shadow entries, so it cannot bypass verified market-data provenance or the reservation seam. Generic journal mutators are private to `pkg/shadow`; production entries use `ReserveTrade`.

## ⚠️ Risk

Previously every strategy/symbol signal derived quantity from its allocation independently. Repeated BUYs could persist open paper positions whose aggregate notional exceeded configured capital, invalidating shadow PnL, VaR, and any promotion decision. A synthetic bridge entry could bypass the same safety boundary.

## 🛡️ Solution

- `Store.ReserveTrade` rejects blank, whitespace-padded, and non-NFC entry identities and validates every legacy identity and status plus OPEN amount, canonical plan/symbol identity, duplicate ID, and duplicate case-insensitive OPEN plan/symbol state before atomically appending only a fitting trade under the existing cross-process journal lock. Legacy identity/status corruption stops the engine before mark-to-market/liquidation mutation.
- Explicit ensemble capital splits require one positive finite fraction per strategy and must sum to exactly one within `1e-9`; malformed budget/split configuration stops before journal, provider, alert, or cycle activity.
- Journal and capital-state replacement propagates a post-rename directory-sync failure; callers fail closed and do not claim a successful reservation/BUY alert without durability confirmation.
- Invalid/open-over-budget records durably latch `*.capital-state.json` with a redacted reason and rebaseline requirement; state-write failure fails closed.
- The engine suppresses BUYs on capacity exhaustion or a durable degradation latch; malformed reservation configuration or persistence faults halt rather than emit a success claim.
- `PromotionEvaluator` validates and latches legacy invalid/open-over-budget state before considering historical closed-trade metrics. It requires a positive finite valuation balance plus finite, bounded gate values; closed PnL aggregates, simulation outputs, and candidate metrics must remain finite. All-win/no-loss samples have undefined profit factor and are non-qualifying. Promotion qualification and its alert side effect use the same journal-locked snapshot, so concurrent journal changes cannot stale eligibility or race a capital-integrity latch. Monte Carlo VaR derives a deterministic seed from the complete trade snapshot, iteration count, and balance, making repeated eligibility and alert metrics reproducible.
- Promotion-candidate alerts do not advertise the unavailable `plan promote` command and explicitly state that they do not activate trading.
- Rebaseline requires explicit CLI acknowledgement and a non-empty canonical journal. It durably archives a copy, persists a pending halt and independent completion-intent marker before replacing the canonical journal, then persists final archive provenance. If clearing the marker cannot be directory-synced, it restores the marker and remains halted; the crash window after a clean completion write but before intent removal also remains halted for audited operator reconciliation rather than automatic resumption. A missing or already-empty journal is refused.
- The orchestrator bridge retains thesis logging but no longer creates a synthetic `ShadowTrade`.
- The one-shot `plan promote` and `plan set` commands are unavailable because their in-memory suite cannot truthfully claim a durable or daemon-applied activation. `AutoPromoEnabled` is also suppressed because its registry is process-local; it emits only a truthful promotion-candidate alert. The legacy `PlanHotSwapper` cannot auto-promote because it has no journal/provenance guard.
- Promotion requires an explicit configured shadow-capital budget distinct from its valuation balance; missing budget fails closed. Nonfinite or overflowing closed PnL/simulation metrics cannot qualify or serialize a candidate. Performance reporting validates the journal and refuses an untrusted leaderboard.

Capital-integrity failures remain observable and non-promotable across restarts until a human performs an auditable clean rebaseline; no live broker action, live order, or market-data/network operation is added.
