## Concrete question and unaccepted claim choice

**Recommended proposed question:** for one separately admitted future observation
epoch, can a fixed PAMR A64 input/decision be traced from its genuinely reviewed
original through a compatible shadow consumer to a complete, bounded simulated
outcome and durable read/reopen record, without duplicate or unauthorized effects,
lost uncertainty, or relabeling simulated facts as broker facts?

This is an **evidence-chain integrity** question, not a test of profitable edge,
general runtime reliability or readiness to promote a strategy. It cannot qualify
the runtime that would admit the case itself; prerequisite qualification remains
separate. A later evidence-complete result would support review of this narrow
case only, not economic eligibility or permission to transact.

The recommendation follows the existing source boundary: PR29 is inert, PR28
rejects its identity, and the actual input/consumer/runtime suppliers remain
unadmitted. Existing evidence can justify exact identity, arithmetic and durable
record criteria. It cannot justify a return cutoff, confidence level, trade count
or observation duration for an economic claim.

**Owner choice remains UNSELECTED:** should the first case address this engineering
evidence question, or economic strategy performance? The latter would ask whether
a precisely frozen adaptation meets an independently justified cost-aware
comparison policy, with adequate prospective evidence. It needs additional
cost/comparator/adequacy/decision-policy evidence; no such numeric criteria are
chosen here. This is the single essential claim decision before this proposed
registration can be accepted, not a request to approve a run or assemble code,
keys or technical evidence. Preparing/reviewing this proposal is already allowed.

## Proposed criteria and why they fit this claim

The following are exact **engineering evidence** requirements, not market
performance thresholds. No criterion is evaluated by this planning document.

1. **Admission precedes observation.** All required case fields, actual supplier
   bindings and applicable protocol R0–R5 evidence are accepted before any run.
   Keep all sixteen original-review checks and their private completion/authority
   conditions. C07/C13/C14 UNKNOWN means this case remains NOT_READY today.
2. **Complete causal identity.** Every expected original/input/transform/artifact/
   decision/consumption/execution/read record has an applicable version, immutable
   parent and allowed time ordering. Compare complete expected and observed sets,
   not matching counts alone; duplicate A cannot stand in for omitted B.
   Retain rejected inputs and no-action decisions. A later receipt cannot prove
   historical availability, and a frozen calendar declaration is not completion.
3. **Exact math and honest rejection.** Record the exact selected pairs and
   representable ratios, initialization and adaptations. Nonterminating,
   out-of-bound, mismatched or stale inputs reject under the accepted policy;
   never round, skip, impute, recapture or select another history to get a result.
   Such a rejection is not an economic loss observation or a successful trade.
4. **Effect and state separation.** No unauthorized dispatch or restored/spent
   capability. Requested terms, observed simulated fills/fees/cash/positions,
   cancellation acknowledgement and settlement remain separate. Retain UNKNOWN
   and corruption fences; clean-looking projections or reopen cannot clear them.
5. **Bounded custody and durable evidence.** Use only an accepted finite operation
   and exposure/copy/lifetime budget with qualified clock/order and complete
   applicable execution/release/own-ack evidence. Source limits—64 rows, 32-character
   ratios, 32,768-byte mathematical requests, 180,000-byte artifacts and the old
   64-record/8-MiB constructed store—are not actual whole-job/handling permission.
   Actual epoch event/byte/time/recipient limits remain UNSELECTED.
6. **Scoped conclusion.** Independently assess the complete applicable record.
   A valid narrow engineering conclusion says nothing about profitable edge,
   adequate strategy observation or a live promotion mechanism. Missing facts
   cannot be promoted to pass to meet an end-of-year date.

The protocol's outcomes remain proposed data assessments, not new wire statuses:
before admission the result is `NOT_READY`; integrity/authority contradiction
blocks positive interpretation; insufficient coverage cannot become a pass;
`REJECTED` requires valid evidence of a predeclared rejection condition; even
`EVIDENCE_COMPLETE_FOR_REVIEW` grants no effect authority. No actual outcome beyond
the present planning disposition is reported here.
