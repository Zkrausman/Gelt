# 🧹 ZAR-202 Closed-Loop Ticket Delivery

## 🎯 What

Establish one required acceptance loop for every ticketed Gelt change, local or Jules: implementation, deterministic verification, fresh independent review, fresh adversarial review, remediation, repeat reviews after every change, completion-specification drafting, fresh final reviews, and final verification.

Jules changes only who performs assigned implementation/remediation and requires parent monitoring; it does not alter acceptance.

## 💡 Why

ZAR-196 revealed process drift: verification after reviewer-driven changes was mistaken for final acceptance without a fresh review of the last diff. The prior Jules packet also limited remediation to one pass.
